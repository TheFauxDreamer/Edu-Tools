Staff Birthday iCal Generator

How to generate the CSV from Compass

  1. Login to Compass and navigate to Waffle → People Management
  2. Select Active Staff on the left
  3. Select the Columns button on the right
  4. Tick both Full Name & Date Of Birth, then the Save button
  5. Click the Export CSV button on the left
