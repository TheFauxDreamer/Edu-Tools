Full Year Subject (40 weeks)

For full year subjects that run across all 4 terms (40 weeks total):

Step 1: Calculate weeks missed before student started

  • Weeks missed from previous terms: (Term number - 1) × 10
  • Weeks missed in current term: Start week - 1
  • Total missed weeks = Previous terms + Current term weeks

Step 2: Calculate discount percentage

  • Discount % = (Missed weeks ÷ 40) × 100%

Step 3: Calculate discount amount

  • Discount Amount = Total Amount × Discount %

Example: Student starting in Term 2, Week 3

  • Previous terms: (2-1) × 10 = 10 weeks missed
  • Current term: 3-1 = 2 weeks missed
  • Total missed: 10 + 2 = 12 weeks
  • Discount: (12 ÷ 40) × 100% = 30%
  • For a $1000 subject: $1000 × 30% = $300 discount
  • Amount to pay: $1000 - $300 = $700

Semester Subject (20 weeks)

For semester subjects that run across 2 terms (20 weeks total):

Step 1: Calculate weeks missed before student started

  • First determine term position within semester:
  • - For Semester 1: Term 1 = position 1, Term 2 = position 2
  • - For Semester 2: Term 3 = position 1, Term 4 = position 2
  • Weeks missed from previous terms: (Term position - 1) × 10
  • Weeks missed in current term: Start week - 1
  • Total missed weeks = Previous terms + Current term weeks

Step 2: Calculate discount percentage

  • Discount % = (Missed weeks ÷ 20) × 100%

Step 3: Calculate discount amount

  • Discount Amount = Total Amount × Discount %

Example: Student starting in Semester 1, Term 2, Week 5

  • Term position in semester: 2
  • Previous terms: (2-1) × 10 = 10 weeks missed
  • Current term: 5-1 = 4 weeks missed
  • Total missed: 10 + 4 = 14 weeks
  • Discount: (14 ÷ 20) × 100% = 70%
  • For a $800 subject: $800 × 70% = $560 discount
  • Amount to pay: $800 - $560 = $240

Term Subject (10 weeks)

Step 3: Calculate discount amount

Example: Student starting in Week 4

Full Year Subject (40 weeks)

For full year subjects that run across all 4 terms (40 weeks total):

Step 1: Calculate weeks remaining after student leaves

  • Weeks remaining in current term: 10 - End week
  • Weeks in remaining terms: (4 - Term number) × 10
  • Total remaining weeks = Current term remaining + Future terms

Step 2: Calculate discount percentage

  • Discount % = (Remaining weeks ÷ 40) × 100%

Step 3: Calculate discount amount

  • Discount Amount = Total Amount × Discount %

Example: Student leaving in Term 2, Week 3

  • Current term remaining: 10 - 3 = 7 weeks
  • Future terms: (4 - 2) × 10 = 20 weeks
  • Total remaining: 7 + 20 = 27 weeks
  • Discount: (27 ÷ 40) × 100% = 67.5%
  • For a $1000 subject: $1000 × 67.5% = $675 discount
  • Amount to pay: $1000 - $675 = $325
